## 🎯 Overview

**JobSync** simplifies the hiring pipeline by connecting applicants with employers. Employers can post vacancies, review resumes, and track candidates across interview stages.

---

## 🚀 Features

* **Role-based Portals**: Separate workflows for Job Seekers and Employers.
* **Application Tracker**: Visual pipeline stages (Applied, Screening, Interview, Offered, Rejected).
* **Search & Filter**: Keyword, salary range, and experience filters for job postings.
